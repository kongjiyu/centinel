Checkout accepts invalid quantities

Business rules

The server accepts orders with quantities outside the allowed range.

Risk HighPriority MediumOpen

Overview

Possible Threats

  1. Invalid order quantities

    Checkout accepts quantities that the business rule excludes. This can create orders that cannot be fulfilled as requested.

  2. Incorrect order totals

    Order creation multiplies the submitted quantity by the unit price. A quantity outside the permitted range can produce an incorrect total.

Recommended Action

Validate each quantity on the server before creating the order. Accept whole numbers from 1 to 10.

Verify that checkout rejects missing values, fractions, and values outside the range. Check that quantities 1 and 10 still succeed and produce the expected totals.
